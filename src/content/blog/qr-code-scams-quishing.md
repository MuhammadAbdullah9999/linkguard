A QR code is just a link in a different shape. You cannot read it with your eyes, which is exactly why scammers use it. Phishing through QR codes is called quishing.

## Where fake QR codes show up

- Stickers placed over real codes on parking meters and charging stations
- Fake restaurant menu or payment codes
- Emails and letters that say you must "scan to verify" an account
- Flyers and posters in public places

## Safe scanning habits

1. **Look at the preview.** Most scanners show the web address before opening it. Read it.
2. **Check for tampering.** If a QR sticker is on top of another, or looks peeled or misaligned, do not use it.
3. **Be wary of urgency.** "Scan now to avoid a fine" is a pressure tactic.
4. **Do not enter logins or card numbers** on a page you reached by QR code unless you are sure of the domain.
5. **Type the address yourself** for anything involving money or accounts.
